# ENTERPRISE COMPLIANCE & AUDIT REMEDIATION PLAYBOOK (SOP-AUD-08)
# Operations & Governance Division

1. OVERVIEW:
This standard operating procedure defines the automated response workflow when an AI agent or auditor detects non-compliant sales transactions or margin violations.

2. VIOLATION CLASSIFICATION & PROTOCOLS:
- Category Alpha (High Risk): Transactions where discount exceeds 20.0% without executive authorization, or operating margin falls below 15.0%.
  Action: Automatically create an Audit Incident Ticket in Jira/ServiceNow, notify Internal Audit, and freeze commission allocation until investigated.
- Category Beta (Medium Risk): Discount between 15.1% and 20.0% missing VP sign-off documentation.
  Action: Issue a formal 5-day documentation request to the regional sales director.
- Category Gamma (Low Risk): Minor date or quota attribution discrepancies under $10,000.

3. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP (HITL) ESCALATION GATE:
Before executing any financial hold, automated notification to the Board Audit Committee, or updating enterprise records, the AI Agent MUST submit a structured remediation proposal and await Human Confirmation (Compliance Officer Approval).
Autonomous ticket drafting is permitted; automated ticket dispatch requires explicit human confirmation.
