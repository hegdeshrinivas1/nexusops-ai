# GLOBAL ENTERPRISE DISCOUNT & PRICING POLICY (DOC-POL-2024-04)
# Effective Date: Q1 2024 | Classification: Confidential Internal

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY
This policy sets the mandatory approval thresholds and commercial parameters for sales transactions across all geographic operating entities (North America, EMEA, APAC, LATAM).

2. STANDARD AUTHORIZATION THRESHOLDS
- Tier 1 (Field Sales Rep): Up to 8.0% standard discount.
- Tier 2 (Regional Sales Director): 8.1% to 15.0% discount.
- Tier 3 (VP of Commercial Operations): 15.1% to 20.0% discount with formal gross-margin justification.
- Tier 4 (Executive Committee / CFO Approval): Any transaction exceeding 20.0% discount MUST have written prior authorization from the CFO or Executive Committee.

3. HARD COMPLIANCE CEILING (CRITICAL)
- Under NO circumstance may a standard enterprise software contract exceed 20.0% aggregate discount without an approved Exception Waiver (Form EW-99).
- Any contract finalized with >= 20.0% discount lacking Form EW-99 is categorized as a Class 1 Financial Control Violation and triggers an automated internal audit and remediation review.
- Target gross margin floor for Enterprise Cloud services is 65.0%. Transactions resulting in operating margins below 45.0% are strictly prohibited.

4. END-OF-QUARTER PRICING ACCELERATORS
- End-of-quarter volume incentives must be pre-registered 14 business days before quarter close.
- Unregistered last-minute concessions to meet quota will not be recognized for commission compensation.
