# MEMORANDUM: Q3 2024 REGIONAL PERFORMANCE & MARGIN EROSION ANALYSIS
# From: Elena Vance, Chief Financial Officer
# To: Executive Leadership Team
# Date: October 14, 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
While overall group revenue in Q3 2024 reached $142.5M (+8.2% YoY), operating margin declined sharply to 18.4% compared to 24.1% in Q3 2023.

REGIONAL FINDINGS:
1. North America (NA):
   - Revenue: $68.2M.
   - Operating Margin: Collapsed from 28.5% to 14.2%.
   - Primary Drivers: Heavy discounting in Tier-1 Strategic Accounts (notably in Cloud Infrastructure deals), aggressive pricing concessions granted in late September, and rising GPU cluster hosting costs.
   - Several deals closed with discounts between 21% and 26%, far exceeding the 20% compliance threshold outlined in DOC-POL-2024-04.

2. EMEA:
   - Revenue: $44.1M.
   - Operating Margin: Remained stable at 23.8%.
   - Healthy pricing discipline maintained across German and UK operations.

3. APAC & LATAM:
   - APAC demonstrated strong 18% YoY growth driven by financial services customers in Singapore and Tokyo.
   - LATAM delivered $9.7M with steady 21.0% margin.

DIRECTIVES & REMEDIATION MANDATE:
- All regional heads must conduct an immediate retrospective audit of any contract with discounts >= 20.0%.
- Commercial teams must deploy automated AI guardrails to flag and prevent unauthorized non-compliant quotes during proposal generation.
